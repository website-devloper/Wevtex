# WEVTEX — Content Rewrite Skill

You rewrite the site's **visible text** so it (1) reads like a confident,
established studio, (2) says something with real meaning — never filler, and
(3) actively earns rankings in Google (SEO) and citations in AI answer engines
(GEO). This is a content pass only: never touch design, components, or logic.

WEVTEX is a Casablanca web, mobile & IT studio: sites, e-commerce, mobile apps,
Google/Meta ads, custom software, automation, WhatsApp chatbots, and SEO.
Language: **French (Morocco)**, "vous", warm but certain.

Apply this skill page by page. For each page, work in this order: keyword →
structure → sentences → SEO check → GEO check → honesty check.

---

## Rule 0 — The honesty guardrail (never break this)

Never invent statistics, review counts, client names, awards, years in business,
or project totals. Fabricated proof is the fastest way to lose trust with humans
AND to be distrusted/ignored by AI answer engines, which cross-check facts.

Where a real fact would strengthen the copy but you don't have it, write a clearly
marked placeholder: `⚠ [nombre réel de projets]`, `⚠ [note Google réelle]`. Never
fill it with a guess. A sentence with a real, modest number beats a sentence with
an impressive fake one — every time.

WEVTEX's honest advantages (write from THESE, not from competitors' scale):
modern stack (Next.js — genuinely fast sites), fast delivery, live clickable
demos anyone can open, a single team from idea to launch, code the client owns.
Do not imply 15 years of history or Fortune-500 clients the studio doesn't have.

## The 5 sentence techniques that make copy read "pro"

Every heading and key paragraph should use at least one. This is the craft that
separates a real agency's copy from template filler.

1. **The contrast sentence ("pas X, mais Y").** Define yourself against something.
   "Un site qui vend — pas seulement un site qui plaît." Memorable because the
   reader can picture the wrong version. Use these liberally; they are the single
   highest-impact device.
2. **Concrete over abstract.** Replace adjectives with specifics. Not "sites
   performants" → "des sites qui chargent en moins de 2 secondes." Not "rapide" →
   "livré en 2 à 4 semaines." A number or a fact beats every adjective.
3. **Confident active verbs.** "Nous construisons / concevons / livrons." Not "nous
   pouvons vous aider à" or "nos solutions permettent de". Short, active, certain.
4. **Result, not activity.** Say what the client GETS, not what you do. Not
   "optimisation SEO on-page" → "vous apparaissez quand vos clients cherchent."
5. **One idea per sentence.** Short sentences read as confident. Cut every "et qui
   permet également de…". If a sentence has two ideas, make it two sentences.

Banned words/phrases (empty filler that signals template copy): "solutions
innovantes", "à la pointe", "sur-mesure premium", "clé en main", "acteur
incontournable", "booster", "leader", "passionné", "nous mettons tout en œuvre".
If you write one, delete it and say the concrete thing instead.

## SEO rules (per page)

1. **One primary keyword per page.** Never target the same keyword on two pages
   (cannibalization). The homepage owns the umbrella term ("agence web maroc");
   each service/city page owns its own exact term.
2. **Keyword placement, naturally:** once in the eyebrow, once in the H1, once in
   the first paragraph, once in a subheading, and in the meta title + description.
   Never force it or repeat it robotically — read the sentence aloud; if it sounds
   stuffed, rewrite it.
3. **One `<h1>` per page** containing the primary keyword. H2/H3 use related terms
   and question phrases.
4. **Every page needs a unique meta title + description.** Title pattern:
   `[Sujet] à [Ville/Maroc] | WEVTEX` (≤60 chars). Description: one benefit-driven
   sentence with the keyword + a CTA (≤155 chars). Write both as part of the copy,
   never as an afterthought.
5. **Internal links in the body:** every service page links to 1–2 related services
   and the relevant city page, using descriptive anchor text (the keyword), never
   "cliquez ici".
6. **City pages get genuinely unique copy** — never a find-and-replace of the city
   name. Write about that city's real business context. Google discounts duplicated
   templates.

## GEO rules — write to be quoted by AI (per page)

AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Google AI Overviews)
quote passages that answer plainly and stand alone. Shape the copy so any
paragraph can be lifted into an AI answer and still make sense.

1. **Answer-first.** Every FAQ answer and blog intro opens with a direct,
   self-contained answer in 1–3 sentences, before any context. Example:
   "Un site vitrine au Maroc coûte généralement entre ⚠ X et ⚠ Y dirhams,
   selon le nombre de pages et les fonctionnalités." A sentence an AI can quote whole.
2. **Facts carry their scope.** "Livré en 2 à 4 semaines." "Sur les ⚠ X avis
   Google de l'agence." Scoped facts read as trustworthy and citable; vague claims
   get ignored.
3. **Self-contained sections.** Each H2 makes sense quoted alone — no "comme vu
   plus haut". Write each section as if it might be the only part someone reads.
4. **State the entity plainly, repeatedly.** Somewhere in plain text on key pages:
   what WEVTEX is (studio web, mobile & IT), where (Casablanca, Maroc), what it
   does, what it costs. AI builds its picture of you from consistent plain
   statements repeated across pages + the schema.
5. **Definitional sentences.** Where relevant, define the concept the way a source
   would: "Le SEO local consiste à…". AI engines quote clean definitions.

## Per-section rewrite direction

### Hero (homepage)
- Eyebrow: the keyword, plain — `Agence web & applications · Casablanca, Maroc`.
- H1: one confident, stance-taking line naming the breadth, primary keyword inside.
  Model: "Du site à l'application, on construit ce qui fait grandir votre activité."
- Subheadline: name the full range in one sentence (sites, e-commerce, apps,
  publicités, solutions sur mesure) + the result. This is where breadth lives —
  NOT the H1.
- Proof strip: ⚠ real numbers only, scoped ("⚠ X projets livrés · ⚠ note Google ·
  livré en 2–4 semaines"). Replace the broken zeros.
- Two CTAs: primary "Demander un devis gratuit", secondary "Voir nos réalisations".
- Keep the H1 as real text, never baked into an image.

### Services overview (homepage)
Each of the 8 lines = a result with an edge, using a contrast where possible.
Not "Optimisation SEO" → "On vous place là où vos clients cherchent — et on y
reste." Each links to its dedicated service page.

### Service pages
Structure per Phase 3 of the SEO brief. First paragraph answers "what is this and
what do I get" directly (GEO). Include a starting price with what affects it
(concrete). 3–5 question FAQ at the bottom. Interlink.

### City pages
Open with the city's real business context, not a generic intro. Frame services
for that city's economy. Unique copy each. Casablanca is the flagship (strongest
relevance — the studio's base).

### Projects
Lean on the honest edge competitors can't match: "Des projets réels, en
production — cliquez, ce sont de vrais sites, pas des maquettes." Keep the
per-project descriptions already written; add the "Résultats illustratifs"
caption once near any metric.

### "Pourquoi WEVTEX" / About
The contrast-sentence section. Four sharp promises, each a "pas X, mais Y":
prix fixe annoncé (pas de rallonge) · code qui vous appartient (partez quand vous
voulez) · une équipe directe (pas d'intermédiaire) · livré en semaines (pas en
mois). This is the highest-craft section — make every line a clean contrast.

### FAQ
Answer-first, self-contained, real facts. FAQPage schema mirrors the visible text.
Cover: prix, délais, propriété, hébergement, SEO, maintenance, technologies, secteurs.

### Footer
Tighten the about blurb to one confident sentence stating the entity plainly
(GEO): "Studio web, mobile & IT basé à Casablanca — sites, applications et
automatisation pour les entreprises marocaines." Keep NAP (nom, adresse,
téléphone) identical to the Google Business Profile.

## Global consistency rules
- "vous" throughout, never "tu". Warm, professional, certain.
- One emphasis word per heading (the lime marker) — never two.
- Sentence case in body; mono uppercase only for eyebrow labels.
- Numbers as digits ("2 à 4 semaines", "moins de 2 secondes") — they catch the eye
  and read as concrete.
- Same phone number everywhere, matching the Google Business Profile.
- Every page's hero text and its `<title>` reinforce the SAME topic/keyword.

## Definition of done (per page)
- Primary keyword present in eyebrow, H1, first paragraph, one subheading, title, description — naturally.
- At least one contrast sentence and zero banned filler words.
- First paragraph / FAQ answers are answer-first and self-contained (GEO).
- All facts scoped; every ⚠ placeholder either filled with real data or removed.
- Unique meta title + description written.
- Internal links use descriptive keyword anchors.
- Reads aloud like a confident human wrote it — not a template.
